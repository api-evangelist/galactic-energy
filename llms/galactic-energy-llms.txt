# Galactic Energy

> Galactic Energy (星河动力航天 — Galactic Energy (Beijing) Space Technology Co., Ltd.) is a private
> Chinese commercial launch company founded on 6 February 2018 and headquartered in Beijing E-Town.
> It flies the Ceres family of solid-propellant small-lift rockets and is developing the partly
> reusable liquid-propellant Pallas family, the Eros vehicle, the Welkin CQ-series engines and the
> Edge attitude and orbital control system. Galactic Energy publishes no API.
